# Business Report

*This file supports your 5-slide `presentation.pdf`. Fill it in first — the slides are a compressed version of this document, not a separate creative exercise.*

## Slide 1 — The Question
*One sentence, in business language, not model language.*
> Example: "Can we estimate a fair asking price for a new listing in under a minute, without waiting on a manual appraisal?"

## Slide 2 — The Approach (30-second version)
*Two sentences max. No hyperparameters, no library names.*
> Example: "We trained a model on 500 past sales, learning the relationship between property size, room count, and location. It was tested against sales it had never seen before."

## Slide 3 — The Result
*The number, translated.*
> Example: "On average, the model's estimate is within $16,000 of the actual sale price — accurate enough to set a starting asking price with confidence."

## Slide 4 — The Limitation
*State it before someone else finds it.*
> Example: "This version hasn't been tested on properties above $2M or below 500 sqft. We recommend manual review for listings outside that range until we can expand the training data."

## Slide 5 — The Recommendation
*What should the business actually do with this?*
> Example: "Use this as a first-pass estimate for standard residential listings under $2M. Route anything outside that range to manual appraisal, and revisit the model in 6 months with new sales data."
